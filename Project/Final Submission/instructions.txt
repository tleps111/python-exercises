
INSTRUCTIONS: 

1. Enter your name and age to begin. You must be at least 12 years old to play.

2. Use the main menu to move between rooms and explore the Land of 8 Kingdoms.

3. Collect available items, including the Sword, Healing Potion, and 12 sacred potions.

4. Check your inventory to see which items you have collected.

5. Save your progress to a JSON file so you can continue your adventure later. You can also load a previously saved game from the main menu.

6. Use a Healing Potion to restore your health when needed.

7. Collect all 12 sacred potions to meet the requirements for the three story routes.

8. Choose your ending:
   - Warrior Route: Reach the Obsidian Throne, collect all 12 sacred potions, have the Sword, and defeat King Sepor in combat.
   - Explorer Route: Reach the Chest of Wisdom with all 12 sacred potions.
   - Diplomat Route: Reach the Obsidian Throne with all 12 sacred potions and seek a peaceful resolution.

9. Watch your health during combat. If your health reaches zero, your adventure ends.

10. Choose Quit from the main menu when you want to exit the game.

MAIN MENU CONTROLS

1. Move to the next room.
2. Collect an item.
3. View your inventory.
4. Save your game.
5. Use a Healing Potion.
6. Fight King Sepor (requires the necessary items and location).
7. Choose a story route.
8. Quit the game.

During combat:
- A = Attack
- D = Defend
- H = Use a Healing Potion

When choosing a story route:
- 1 = Warrior
- 2 = Explorer
- 3 = Diplomat

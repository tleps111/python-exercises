

Welcome to The Game (of Thrones)!

You are Tortan, a knight who must save the Land of 8 Kingdoms from the cruel King Sepor, 
who rules with dark magic and an army of undead.

With the help of the white mage Forika, collect all 12 sacred potions 
and choose your path: defeat Sepor, explore the Chest of Wisdom, or seek peace through diplomacy.

The future of the kingdom is in your hands. Choose your path wisely!

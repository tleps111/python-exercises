#  THE GAME (OF THRONES)

## Game Idea:

I originally wrote the game story for the English Communication lesson and decided to build it around this story.  

## Overview

The Game (of Thrones) is a command-line text-based adventure game set in the Land of 8 Kingdoms. The player takes the role of a knight named Tortan who must help save the 8 Kingdoms from the cruel King Sepor and his dark magic.

## Objective

The objective is to progress through the kingdoms, explore different locations, collect items and find a way to resolve the conflict with King Sepor.

This game offers 3 different routes:

    - Warrior route: Fight King Sepor and defeat him.
    - Explorer route: Collect all 12 sacred potions and reach the Chest of Wisdom.
    - Diplomat route: Collect all 12 sacred potions and negotiate peace with King Sepor.

## Operating Principles and Functionalities

The game is played through a command-line interface. At the beginning, the player enters their name and age. Players must be at least 12 years old to play. The main menu allows the player to choose different actions throughout the adventure.

The player moves between rooms in the eight kingdoms, where they can find and collect items. The inventory shows the items collected and their weight. Items have different purposes: the Sword is needed to fight King Sepor, while the Healing Potion restores the player's health.

The game includes a combat system where the player can attack King Sepor, defend themselves or use a Healing Potion. The player's health and Sepor's health determine the outcome of the battle.

There are three different ways to complete the adventure. For the Warrior route, the player must collect all 12 sacred potions, obtain the Sword, reach the Obsidian Throne and defeat King Sepor. For the Explorer route, the player must collect all 12 sacred potions and reach the Chest of Wisdom. For the Diplomat route, the player must collect all 12 sacred potions and reach the Obsidian Throne to negotiate peace with King Sepor.

The game also includes save and load functionality. Players can save their progress and continue playing later. Each player has a separate JSON save file based on their name. If a player starts a new game and a save file already exists, the game asks for confirmation before overwriting it.

## Sustainable Development Goal

The Game (of Thrones) takes into account the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions. 

This goal is reflected in the diplomat route, where the main character, Tortan, tries to resolve the conflict with the tyrant King Sepor peacefully instead of fighting him. This shows that negotiation and cooperation can help end wars and conflicts and create peace between countries.
